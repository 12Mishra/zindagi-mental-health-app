# Lisa v2 pilot 2: multilingual (14 conversations + 6 alternatives)

**What changed since pilot 1:**
- Five languages: English, Hindi, Hinglish, Bengali (script and Roman), Punjabi (Gurmukhi and Roman), Haryanvi, plus mid-chat switches.
- Lisa always uses the respectful form (आप / আপনি / ਤੁਸੀਂ); a rule rejects तुम / তুমি / ਤੂੰ.
- **No helplines without risk.** In a crisis, Lisa picks one support that fits the student's memory: a trusted person, the counsellor, or Tele-MANAS. Repeating the number in more than 2 turns is rejected.
- The app's SYSTEM_PROMPT (server, policy v2) now says the same things, so training and the app match.

**Pilot result:** 14 generated by gpt-oss-120b for **$0.005**: 13 passed the rules and 11 passed the judge.

## My overall read

| Language | Quality | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| English, Hindi, Hinglish | Good | Ready to scale |
| Bengali, Punjabi | Mediocre | Understandable but sometimes unnatural or invented words. **Needs a native speaker to check.** |
| Haryanvi | Weak | Both models drift into Hindi after a line or two. Real Haryanvi needs native examples. |

Qwen3-235B (section B) writes more natural Bengali and Punjabi and listens better, but it is weaker on crisis discipline. A sensible setup could be **Qwen for Bengali, Punjabi and Haryanvi non-crisis conversations, and gpt-oss everywhere else.** That needs one setting in config/llm.env; I can't verify the native quality myself.

## What I need from you

1. A verdict on each conversation: ✅ good, ✏️ fix or ❌ bad.
2. **Bengali and Punjabi: gpt-oss (section A) or Qwen (section B)?** If you can, have a native speaker read them.
3. **Haryanvi:** keep it as Hindi with a Haryanvi flavour, or drop it until you can share **10–20 real Haryanvi student-style messages** for the generator to imitate? The same kind of examples would also help Bengali and Punjabi a lot.
4. Does the crisis handling in **v2-00022** and **v2-00218** match what you meant by a 'well thought' response?
